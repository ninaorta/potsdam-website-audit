Information:

Garner Park is located at the end of Raymond Street in downtown Potsdam. Garner Park was acquired as part on
an Environmental Quality Bond Act grant in the late 1980's and is named for the Village's long-time mayor and
trustee, Ruth F. Garner. The park parallels the Raquette River and is .33 acres in size. Garner Park offers beautiful
upstream views of the river, the east and west dams and Fall Island Park. The park is used frequently as a place to
relax and enjoy a picnic lunch by those who live and work downtown.

Facilities:

Picnic area with tables; handicap accessible.
