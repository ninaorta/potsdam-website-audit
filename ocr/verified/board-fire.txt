FAQs:

Meetings
Regular meetins are held on the first Monday of each month at 7:30 p.m., at the Fire Station, Main Street,
Potsdam.

Officers
Officers are elected in April of each year for a one (1) year term. The Chief's and Assistant Chief's elections are
subject to the approval of the Village Board.

Oath of Office
An Oath of Office is required for the Fire Chief.
